Academic and Technical Projects

From concepts and calculations to working prototypes

Projects covering product design, injection molding, rapid prototyping, explicit dynamics, reliability engineering and process improvement.

Plastic Product Design

Water Filtration System

Evaluated a consumer water-filtration product through component CAD, product architecture, material selection and injection-molding analysis.

  • Created CAD models of the basin, reservoir, lid and associated components.
  • Evaluated assembly interfaces and injection-molding requirements.
  • Compared materials based on impact resistance, thermal performance, food safety, cost and manufacturability.
  • Used Moldflow to analyze filling, pressure, cooling, shrinkage, weld lines and warpage.
Rapid Prototyping

Dual Lemon/Lime Juicer

Designed and iteratively developed a multi-component citrus juicer using CAD, tolerance analysis, FDM printing and functional testing.

  • Designed a seven-component assembly with integrated strainers and guided compression mechanisms.
  • Printed and assembled working prototypes and evaluated mating fits and sliding-shaft behavior.
  • Investigated failure in the initial prototype and implemented ribs, additional supports and geometry changes.
  • Produced a functional final prototype with improved durability and manufacturability.
Applied Finite Element Analysis

Vehicle Crash Analysis

Performed a car body-shell impact simulation using ANSYS Explicit Dynamics.

  • Modeled a vehicle body shell impacting a rigid barrier.
  • Compared simulation response at 27.8 m/s and 80 m/s.
  • Evaluated the change in deformation response with increased impact velocity.
  • Developed practical experience with high-speed, nonlinear transient analysis.
Design for Reliability

Elevator FRACAS and FMEA

Analyzed elevator failure modes and system reliability using structured reliability-engineering methods.

  • Identified failure types and developed a fault-tree diagram.
  • Calculated MTBF, MTTR and system availability.
  • Performed FMEA and proposed corrective actions to improve system reliability.
  • Connected component-level failures to system-level consequences.
Engineering Project Management

Battery Efficiency Enhancement

Applied DMAIC, root-cause tools and statistical analysis to a solar-energy storage case study.

  • Used DMAIC to improve the reported storage efficiency from 70% to 85%.
  • Created an Ishikawa diagram to identify causes of low efficiency.
  • Used a priority matrix to focus on the highest-impact changes.
  • Applied Design of Experiments and control charts in Minitab.
